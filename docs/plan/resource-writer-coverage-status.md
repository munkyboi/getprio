## Evidence vocabulary

- **Integrated:** the selected writer has a scoped transaction/revision adapter and relevant tests in source. This does not certify every caller, bypass writer, held grant, historical record or live behavior.
- **Gap:** a concrete missing transaction, revision or provenance guarantee is visible in the inspected path.
- **Unproven:** source or evidence is insufficient to attest the guarantee; further tracing or execution is required.
- **Excluded narrowly:** the identified operation does not change resource demand, occupancy, dispatch or source eligibility. Its authorization, delivery or privacy requirements still apply. Mixed operations must be split into their relevant effects.

## Reconciliation and readiness prerequisites

The [source analysis](../../backend/src/services/resourceSourceAssessmentAnalysis.js) still returns null configuration/plan watermarks and `not_ready`/null snapshot. The [capacity foundation](../../database/migrations/20261003_add_resource_capacity_foundation.sql) constrains tracking to false; the [ledger foundation](../../database/migrations/20261007_add_resource_ledger_foundation.sql) constrains writer coverage to false. These are inspected source constraints, not a fresh production database query.

Before complete B3 assembly, the runtime owner must establish all of the following:

1. Enumerate applicable HTTP, callback, worker, lazy-read, internal, script, migration, cleanup and administrative writers; specify actor/system scope, lock order, same-client writes, held grants, retries, no-op and revision effects. Close concrete gaps and classify exclusions individually.
2. Define and implement monotonic configuration, plan and Queue Day/segment provenance, including deletion, recreation and visibility/tombstone semantics. Existing ledger revisions, timestamps and content fingerprints are not sufficient substitutes.
3. Reconcile missing/stale/unknown plans, unsupported composition, ambiguous lineage, unused terminal protection, converted reservations, timing-open work without allocation evidence and prior-day held allocations. Preserve every unknown/unsupported item; do not drop work to claim completeness or repair it by assumption.
4. Establish actual dispatch eligibility/order and complete requested-horizon inventory under coherent bounded authorized reads. Equal tuples, concurrent changes, access revocation, row/byte bounds and unavailable storage must fail closed.
5. Define separately reviewed scope activation/repair-or-exclusion policy with invalidation and rollback evidence. This document authorizes neither repair nor relaxing database constraints.

B1 remains internal-only: [resourceSourceAssessment](../../backend/src/services/resourceSourceAssessment.js) has no live diagnostic consumer at this checkpoint. Deployment/health or a release-readiness smoke does not establish that integration. B3 cannot turn report rows into a ready envelope while these prerequisites remain open. B4's trusted-byte adapter, private capture/privacy, actual-start evaluation and customer pilots retain independent gates. Public time-to-call baseline, tracked pool enforcement and multi-stage journey operation are separate capabilities.
