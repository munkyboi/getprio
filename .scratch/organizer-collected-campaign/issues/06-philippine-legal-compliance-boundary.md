# Philippine legal and compliance boundary

Type: research
Status: resolved
Claimed by: Codex (/root)
Blocked by: 02

## Question

What Philippine payment-system, securities/crowdfunding, privacy, consumer-protection, e-commerce, tax, and dispute-handling obligations or product boundaries apply to organizer-collected campaign instructions, payment proofs, reimbursement records, public discovery, and ratings—and which features would move GetPrio into a regulated payment or crowdfunding role?

## Known direction

- GetPrio does not hold, transmit, settle, or automatically reimburse contributor money.
- The organizer publishes payment instructions and reviews proof; vendor users do not participate.
- Contribution and reimbursement evidence is private and role-scoped.
- Public campaigns are optional and ratings have a public vendor-review component.

## Research asset

[Philippine compliance boundary research brief](../../../docs/research/2026-07-18-organizer-collected-campaign-philippine-compliance.md)

## Resolution

The organizer-collected direction is viable only as a record-only private collection feature, subject to qualified Philippine counsel's release sign-off. GetPrio must not hold, transmit, initiate, settle, pool, automatically reimburse, or take a transfer-based fee from contributor money without a BSP-scope assessment. It must not market or structure campaigns as investment, lending, profit-sharing, ownership, or securities crowdfunding without SEC advice.

The release gate requires DTI Internet Transactions Act/Trustmark applicability confirmation, campaign-specific consumer disclosures and redress, a DPA privacy notice/PIA/retention and evidence-access control set, and Philippine tax/accounting advice. Ratings require moderation, dispute exclusion from aggregates, and no automatic eligibility block in v1.

## Resolution comment

Resolved from current BSP, SEC, NPC, and DTI primary sources. The linked brief distinguishes the regulatory source from product recommendations and leaves formal legal/tax conclusions to qualified counsel.
